Model & validation

        

          Steady state, complete mixing, bound oxygen only and prescribed oxygen
          consumption. Conservation checks and cross-runtime agreement verify
          the numerical implementation; they do not establish clinical validity.
        

        
What the equations assume

        

          A common mixed arterial content supplies pulmonary and systemic paths.
          Pulmonary venous saturation Spv is an explicit endpoint. Qt = Qp + Qs;
          oxygen capacity B = κ × Hb or an explicit direct capacity. In
          consistent volume units, Ca = B × Spv − M/Qp, Cv = Ca − M/Qs, and
          systemic oxygen delivery is Qs × Ca. Pulmonary net oxygen uptake
          equals the prescribed consumption M; gross pulmonary oxygen flux is a
          different quantity.
        

        

          The implementation includes the 100 mL/dL and 1,000 mL/L conversions
          explicitly. Per-kg flows use mL blood/kg/min and oxygen fluxes mL
          O₂/kg/min. Per-m² flow uses L blood/min/m² and oxygen flux mL
          O₂/min/m². Resistance experiments use L blood/min, mmHg and
          mmHg·min/L; normalized source indices have units L/min × saturation,
          not physical mL O₂/min.
        

        

          Negative venous content is masked as no admissible steady state at the
          requested consumption. The Cv = 0 boundary remains a distinct status.
          Undefined zero-demand ratios remain null. Sa/Sv criteria are
          separately selected strict inequalities; equality is not above a
          criterion. No saturation clipping or automatic demand adjustment
          occurs.

          Analytical boundaries, Hb gains, optima, nonlinear shunt resistance,
          circuit-secant output closure and declared-distribution paired
          ensembles are explicit derivations or sensitivity extensions.
          Resistance profiles describe assumptions; they are not drug doses or
          clinical efficacy rankings. The output-law anchor and shunt
          calibration are frozen according to each experiment's declared
          reference policy.
        

        
Excluded physiology and use

        

          No patient-specific prediction, treatment target or dosing
          recommendation is produced. The model excludes pulsatility and time
          dynamics, ventricular mechanics, oxygen dissociation and dissolved
          oxygen, viscosity feedback, autoregulation, collateral flows and
          supply-dependent consumption. A caval sample is not necessarily ideal
          mixed venous blood. Hb changes here hold the displayed flows and
          demand fixed.
