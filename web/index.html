Model & validation

        

          Complete mixing, steady state, bound oxygen only, prescribed oxygen
          consumption, common arterial content and pulmonary venous endpoint.
          This model excludes patient-specific prediction, time dynamics,
          dissolved oxygen, viscosity feedback and supply-dependent consumption.
        

        

          Passing conservation and browser-parity tests is numerical
          verification, not clinical validation. Ahmed full text and current
          Savorgnan access remain unresolved; no exact-source replication is
          claimed.
